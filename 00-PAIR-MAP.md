## 1. The pair

1. `Non-Causal-Non-Causality.md` — the formal paper. It types a source-attributed constitutive boundary, its exact compilation into structural admissibility, a sealed construction specification, a future-indexed reservoir, its prefix–fibre geometry and present-time projection, its history-generated recurrence across updates, the separation between generation, reading, selection and enforcement, and the platform-level instantiation of this architecture for a particular person or system.
2. `Reality-Catches-the-Predicate.md` — the bridge. It develops the same object in phenomenological and architectural language: reality does not receive a message from the future; the present is admitted as the first section of a future-compatible trajectory space constructed upstream.

The bridge does not add mathematical claims beyond the formal paper. The formal paper does not treat the bridge's metaphors as premises.

## 3. New object isolated by this pair

The pair isolates a new architectural object without yet declaring an additional primitive in the NC2.5 axiomatic core. It composes functions that were distributed across the corpus:

> a future-indexed admissibility environment maintained at declared updates, whose present interface exposes only typed projections of trajectories that remain compatible with declared identity, structural admissibility and horizon-relative viability.

The object is provisionally denoted

\[
\mathfrak{A}_{u,t}^{H}

(B_{u,t}^{v_B},
A_{u,t,0:H},
\Theta_{u,t}^{H},
\mathcal{R}_{u,t}^{H},
\mathcal G(\mathcal R_{u,t}^{H}),
\Pi^{\exists,H}_{u,t},
\Pi^{\mathrm{rob},H}_{u,t},
\mathsf K,
\Pi_{\Sigma}^{\mathrm{rob}},
\mathsf K^{\Sigma},
\mathsf{Cert},
\mathsf{Val},
\mathsf{Gen},
\mathsf{Upd},
\Sigma,
O_{\mathrm{view}},
O_{\mathrm{opt}},
O_{\mathrm{enf}},
G_{\mathrm{enf}}).
\]

`Atmosphere` is the bridge name for this maintained tuple. The formal object is not a substance, a reward, an oracle or a physical future.

`B`, `A` and `\mathcal G(\mathcal R)` are exact typed views already bound by `\Theta` and `\mathcal R`: listing them keeps the five construction nodes visible without creating parallel mutable stores or a second ground.

| Link | Typed map | Function |
|---|---|---|
| `B → A` | `\mathsf{compile}(B)` | Conjoin the executable personal and external source clauses carried by `B`. |
| `A → \mathcal R` | `\mathsf{filter}(h_t,H,\Theta[I,A,V,\mathsf{Dyn},\mathsf{Val}])` | Retain exactly the reachable tails satisfying identity, compiled admissibility and prefix viability under held construction inputs. |
| `\mathcal R → \mathcal G(\mathcal R)` | exact prefix–fibre derivation | Enumerate the complete prefix-indexed fibre family of the sealed reservoir. |
| `\mathcal G(\mathcal R) → \Pi^{\exists}` | `\mathsf{First}`, for `H\ge1` | Project the first actions of the one-step fibres. |

Adaptive and seal-relative robust supports are not unary outputs of geometry. They additionally bind history or realised prefix, declared disturbances and successors, specification lineage, recursive continuation evidence and, in seal-relative mode, the original seal.

Across updates, valid instances are joined by the history-generated admissibility recurrence: authenticated observation and unique interpretation → source-bound specification and pre-action seal → active support → selector proposal → authenticated enforcement decision → declared event → exact history append → successor observation and interpretation → provenance-bound successor specification → recomputed successor seal. This joining relation is part of the pair's object and is separately falsifiable; recurrence alone does not establish adaptive or original-seal robustness.

## 4. Load-bearing separations

The following distinctions may not be collapsed during drafting:

1. **Trajectory predicate / extension.** A predicate classifies trajectories; the reservoir is the reachable extension satisfying it.
2. **Future-indexed object / future event.** The reservoir is constructed at time `t` and indexed by later times. No event in the future transmits information backward.
3. **Ground / interface.** The privileged ground is non-actionable only in an authority- and channel-relative sense: task processes may consume derived projections but cannot directly query, rewrite or treat the ground as their objective. Exact non-reconstructibility remains conditional on a declared target-changing fibre collision.
4. **Reading / selection / enforcement.** A consumer may read a typed projection without receiving authority to revise the predicate, select the final task action or execute a transition.
5. **Identity / budget.** `I_u` and its witness `\mathsf W_u` are not functions of `\Phi_{\mathrm{load}}` or `\tau_b` without an additional bridge premise.
6. **Existential / adaptive-robust / seal-relative robust continuation.** One possible continuation does not guarantee that every declared successor remains in a current-reservoir fibre with recursively available regenerated continuation under one fixed construction-specification lineage; that adaptive guarantee does not prove fidelity to the original sealed reservoir. A changed specification starts a new adaptive certificate. Adaptive and recursively seal-relative supports need not be nested without an additional consistency premise: the first recurses through regenerated reservoirs, while the second recurses inside the original seal.
7. **Admissibility / liveness.** Non-emptiness of the reservoir is an existential viability verdict, not IIC cycle-reinitiation liveness without a separate theorem.
8. **Personal source / external source / platform composition.** A person or another authorised constitutive process supplies personal clauses; shared, legal, physical, safety and platform clauses retain their own authorities; the platform supplies typed composition, isolation, provenance and enforcement separation. None of these facts grants optimizer write-access to the boundary.
9. **Prefix–fibre geometry / scalar summary.** A Boolean verdict or scalar summary may describe a reservoir but does not determine its branching, action fibres, prefix fibres or order-sensitive support whenever two reservoirs collide under that summary. This is collision-relative; it is not a claim that every scalar fails on every restricted class.
10. **Structural novelty / classical machinery.** Backward reachability, viability kernels and receding-horizon projection are classical neighbours. The candidate contribution is their authority-typed, identity-relative, channel-confined and platform-instantiated composition within NC2.5, together with explicit reconstruction falsifiers.
11. **Recomputed reservoir / sealed conditional fibre.** Non-emptiness of a newly generated successor reservoir does not prove that the realised prefix remains in the prefix closure of the original sealed reservoir, equivalently that it indexes a non-empty original-seal fibre. The latter requires recursive seal-relative robust support and its `K^Sigma` continuation indicator.
12. **Observation / interpretation.** Authenticated telemetry is not yet an attributed history. A versioned partial interpreter must either produce one unique history with provenance or fail without constructing a reservoir.
13. **Assurance evidence / enforcement verdict.** Existential, adaptive-robust and seal-relative evidence are a tagged union, not one generic certificate. The enforcement channel must authenticate that full context and the executor must reject forged, stale, replayed or wrongly bound decisions.
14. **Reservoir non-emptiness / active-support non-emptiness.** A reservoir may contain favourable tails while the selected robust support is empty. Empty semantics are therefore keyed to `\Pi^{\mathrm{act}}=\varnothing`, not only to `\mathcal R=\varnothing`.
15. **Type separation / process topology.** Generator, consumer, selector and enforcer are distinct authority types. The paper does not prove that each must occupy a separate physical process.
16. **Representation / ground.** A reservoir represents currently certified continuations. It does not create the independent ground of identity, load, admissibility or viability merely by storing their verdicts.
17. **Construction specification / task objective.** `\Theta_{u,t}^{H}` binds horizon, declaration, the attributed interpreter, admissibility, viability, dynamics, model, provenance and their authority/version records. It is not supplied by the task goal, and a later `\Theta` cannot discharge an earlier continuation certificate.
18. **History-generated admissibility recurrence / physical retrocausality.** Accumulated history plus a current valid specification indexes the next admissible future fibre; admitted execution and exact append create the next history. The relation is a strict chain of growing prefixes, not a future event acting on the past and not history alone determining the realised future.
19. **Prefix of an admitted tail / prefix-level joint verdict.** Theorem 4.6 preserves a non-empty full-tail extension and prefix-closure membership. It does not imply `I_u(h_t\oplus\rho_k)=1`, `A_{u,t,k}(h_t,\rho_k)=1` or `J_{u,t}^{k}(h_t,\rho_k)=1`; those stronger claims require the explicit thin temporal restriction premise `(TR)`.
20. **Constitutive boundary / compiled admissibility.** `B_{u,t}^{v_B}` records the personal and external source clauses, their authorities, composition, version and provenance; `A_{u,t,0:H}` is the executable conjunction compiled from that source map. A mismatch invalidates the specification. `B` is one sealed view inside the declaration/specification, not a second mutable store.
21. **Unrealised future tail / present action fibre.** No tail must occur to affect existential support. Two present, authorised `B/A` specifications can retain or empty the whole reachable fibre beginning with action `a`; the resulting support difference is counterfactual model dependence at time `t`, not a future event signalling backward.

## 5. Required falsification surface

Before either text may be described as closed, it must expose at least the following failure conditions:

- the reservoir is fitted after the realised outcome rather than sealed before action;
- the claimed atmosphere has no intervention-sensitive effect on any declared consumer channel;
- changing only an authenticated enforcement verdict in an otherwise matched complete-mediation case leaves the realised execution trace unchanged;
- changing the personal trajectory predicate leaves the reservoir invariant on a non-degenerate test class;
- the source, subject or version carried by `B` does not match the compiled `A` record sealed in the same construction specification;
- two authorised personal `B` versions retain and empty an otherwise unmasked reachable action fibre, yet the existential support does not change;
- equal-cardinality reservoirs with different first-action supports are treated as the same prefix–fibre geometry;
- changing the task objective changes the privileged boundary rather than only selection within admitted support;
- a consumer reconstructs the protected predicate from its permitted transcript beyond the declared bound;
- the existential reservoir is advertised as adaptive-robust without universal disturbance-successor quantification, a non-empty current one-step fibre and a recursive continuation indicator evaluated under one fixed construction-specification lineage;
- a later changed construction specification is substituted to rescue an earlier adaptive certificate;
- the realised prefix is no longer a prefix of any trajectory in the sealed reservoir without a declared shock, model revision, validity-envelope expiration, predicate revision, selection or enforcement failure, or construction, certification or seal-integrity failure;
- recomputed successor reservoirs remain non-empty while the original-seal conditional fibre indexed by the realised prefix is already empty, or a shallow original-seal fibre remains non-empty while no full-horizon seal-relative strategy exists;
- a prefix of an admitted horizon-`H` tail is promoted to a horizon-`k` identity, admissibility or joint verdict without a sealed `(TR)` audit, or a terminal-only counterexample fails to block that stronger claim;
- reservoir non-emptiness is called life without an explicit bridge to the chosen liveness target;
- `\Phi_{\mathrm{load}}` or `\tau_b` is used as the identity witness;
- the platform silently substitutes a common value table for the person's declared constitutive content;
- the text claims that a separately deployed reservoir service is logically necessary even though an integrated, trajectory-grounded gate preserves the same typed boundaries and invariants;
- a recurrence link omits pre-action sealing, exact append, successor interpretation, prior-seal/event provenance or independent successor-reservoir recomputation, or presents the growing-prefix relation as literal future-to-past causation.
